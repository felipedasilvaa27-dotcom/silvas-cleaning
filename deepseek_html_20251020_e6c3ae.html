Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

What's included in your standard cleaning service? +
Our standard cleaning includes dusting all surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors, cleaning bathrooms (toilets, showers, sinks), kitchen cleaning (countertops, sink, appliance exteriors), and general tidying up.
Do I need to provide cleaning supplies? +
No, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. We use eco-friendly products that are safe for your family and pets. If you have specific products you prefer, we're happy to use them.
How long does a typical cleaning take? +
The duration depends on the size of your space and the service selected. A standard cleaning for an average 3-bedroom home typically takes 2-3 hours, while deep cleaning can take 4-6 hours.
Are your cleaners insured and background checked? +
Yes, all our cleaners are thoroughly background checked, fully insured, and trained in our cleaning methods. Your safety and security are our top priorities.
